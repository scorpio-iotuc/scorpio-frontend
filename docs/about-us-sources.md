## Supplied research material

- `Avanza_UC_2025__SCORPIO-final.pdf`: page 1 supplies investigator roles and
  affiliations; pages 2–4 establish the objective and methodology. The proposal
  connects the work with FLoRaSAT, UFSC simulation tools and DORSAL-IoT.
  Proposed outcomes are not presented as completed results.
- `Presentación Scorpio (1).pdf`: pages 7–11 establish the team; pages 29–32
  assign the antenna, software and power work. The antenna bands are
  137, 433 and 915 MHz. Pages 33–34 describe preliminary NTN testing; no
  performance claims from these slides are asserted on the About Us page.
- `IPre_Informe_SCORPIO_PPZT (1).docx`: abstract and methodology describe the
  edge-to-cloud infrastructure, local buffering, synchronization and website.
  The prototype is a foundation for measurement campaigns, not proof of a
  completed geographically distributed deployment.
- `Informe SCORPIO.pdf`: page 1 identifies Santiago Arntz and describes his
  Doppler and pass-tracking research. Doppler correction results are inconclusive;
  the biography describes the research topic without claiming successful correction.
- The user's current instructions supersede the historical student status of
  Pedro Zavala: graduate researcher and current SCORPIO maintainer.
- Nationalities were supplied by the user as Chilean, Brazilian and French;
  mapped to the UC members, Richard Demo Souza and Juan A. Fraire respectively.
  They are stored separately from the institution's country.
